DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION VIJAYANAGARA, KARNATAKA
CITIZEN CHARTER & PUBLIC SERVICES DIRECTORY

1. District Overview:
Vijayanagara is the 31st district of Karnataka, carved out from Ballari district on 2nd October 2021, with its administrative headquarters at Hosapete.
The district comprises six administrative taluks:
1. Hosapete (District HQ)
2. Harapanahalli
3. Kudligi
4. Huvina Hadagali
5. Hagaribommanahalli
6. Kottur

2. Key Administrative & Welfare Offices:

4. Citizen Service Delivery Centers (CSC / Grama One):
- Grama One and Common Service Centres operate across all gram panchayats in Vijayanagara District.
  Services Offered:
  * Seva Sindhu application submission (Gruha Lakshmi, Yuva Nidhi, Gruha Jyothi).
  * Ration Card application, name addition/deletion, e-KYC.
  * Revenue department certificates (Caste, Income, Residence, Birth, Death).
  * Aadhaar update and biometric authentication.
  * PM-Kisan e-KYC and farmer registration.
  * Electricity and utility bill payments (GESCOM).
