GOVERNMENT OF INDIA & GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA
National Health Authority & Suvarna Arogya Suraksha Trust (SAST)
POLICY MANUAL: AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY) - AROGYA KARNATAKA

1. Scheme Overview:
Ayushman Bharat - Arogya Karnataka (AB-ArK) is a co-branded universal health assurance scheme providing comprehensive cashless treatment coverage to citizens of Karnataka for secondary and tertiary healthcare conditions.

2. Financial Coverage:
- Eligible Category (BPL / AAY / SECC Beneficiaries): Up to Rs. 5,00,000 (Five Lakhs) per family per year on a family floater basis for specified surgical and medical treatments across empaneled public and private hospitals.
- General Category (APL Families): 30% co-payment coverage by government up to Rs. 1,50,000 per family per year.

3. Eligibility:
- Residents of Karnataka possessing a valid Ration Card (BPL or APL) or listed in the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC) database.
- Must hold an Ayushman Bharat Health Card (ABHA / Ayushman Card).

4. Access to Healthcare & Referral Procedure:
- Primary Care & Emergency: Patients can directly access government health facilities (PHC, CHC, Taluk Hospital Hosapete, Harapanahalli, Kudligi).
- Secondary & Tertiary Care: Patient must visit the nearest Taluk General Hospital or District Hospital. If treatment is not available, an official online referral (SAST Referral Code) is generated to empaneled private hospitals.
- Emergency Cases: Direct admission without prior referral is permitted in empaneled hospitals for life-threatening medical emergencies.

5. Key Documents:
- Aadhaar Card of each family member.
- BPL / Antyodaya Ration Card issued by Food & Civil Supplies Department.
- Ayushman Card (can be generated at CSC / Grama One / Arogya Mitra counter).

6. Service Network in Vijayanagara District:
- District Hospital Hosapete, Taluk Hospital Harapanahalli, Kudligi Taluk Hospital, Community Health Centre Hadagali, Hagaribommanahalli, Kottur.
- Arogya Mitra Help Desks are stationed 24/7 at all government general hospitals to guide citizens.
- Official Portals: https://pmjay.gov.in and https://sast.karnataka.gov.in
