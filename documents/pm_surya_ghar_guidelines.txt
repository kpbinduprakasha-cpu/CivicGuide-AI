# PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (Central Government Scheme)
# Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India
# Official Portal: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in | Launch Year: 2024 | Jurisdiction: All India (Central Government)

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE AND OVERVIEW
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a flagship national initiative by the Central Government aimed at providing free electricity up to 300 units per month to 1 crore (10 million) households across all States and Union Territories in India by installing rooftop solar systems.

2. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AND SUBSIDY STRUCTURE
Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, the Central Government provides direct financial subsidy (Direct Benefit Transfer) into the beneficiary's bank account:
- 1 kW Rooftop Solar System: Central Financial Assistance (CFA) / Subsidy of Rs. 30,000.
- 2 kW Rooftop Solar System: Central Financial Assistance (CFA) / Subsidy of Rs. 60,000.
- 3 kW or above Rooftop Solar System: Central Financial Assistance (CFA) / Subsidy of Rs. 78,000 (Maximum cap).
- Special Category States/UTs (North-Eastern States, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep): Higher subsidy rates apply up to Rs. 85,800 for 3 kW systems.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Must be an Indian citizen and resident of any State or Union Territory in India.
- The applicant household must own a residential house with a suitable roof space that is structurally sound and unshaded for solar panel installation.
- The household must have a valid domestic (residential) grid electricity connection in their name from the local DISCOM (Electricity Distribution Company, e.g., GESCOM, BESCOM, BSES, Tata Power, Mahavitaran, UPPCL, WBSEDCL).
- The household must not have availed any previous solar rooftop subsidy from central or state government for the same premises.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Aadhaar Card of the electricity consumer/applicant.
- Latest domestic electricity bill (showing Consumer Account Number / CA Number / Account ID).
- Bank account passbook or cancelled cheque (Aadhaar linked for DBT transfer).
- Electricity Connection proof / Property ownership proof / Land certificate.
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar.

5. APPLICATION PROCEDURE
Step 1: Register on the National Portal at https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in by selecting your State, Electricity Distribution Utility (DISCOM), and entering your Consumer Account Number.
Step 2: Submit the application for Rooftop Solar with your electricity bill details.
Step 3: Receive technical feasibility approval from your local DISCOM.
Step 4: Install the rooftop solar plant through any registered/empanelled solar vendor in your state.
Step 5: Vendor submits installation report and applies for net-metering with the DISCOM.
Step 6: DISCOM inspects the site, installs the bidirectional net meter, and issues a commissioning certificate.
Step 7: Submit bank account details on the national portal to receive the subsidy directly via DBT within 30 days.

6. CITIZEN BENEFITS
- Generates 300 to 360 units of clean electricity per month for an average 3 kW plant, saving approximately Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 24,000 annually on electricity bills.
- Surplus electricity generated can be sold back to the local DISCOM through net-metering.
- Low-interest collateral-free loans (around 7% interest rate) available through public and private sector banks for the non-subsidized portion.
