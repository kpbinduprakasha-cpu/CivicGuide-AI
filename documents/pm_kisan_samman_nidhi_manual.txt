GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES: PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)

1. Scope and Scheme Benefits:
PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme to supplement financial requirements of landholding farmers for procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.
- Annual Income Support: Rs. 6,000 per family per year.
- Mode of Payment: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the verified Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of beneficiaries in three equal quarterly installments of Rs. 2,000 each (April-July, August-November, December-March).

2. Beneficiary Definition and Eligibility:
- Eligible Beneficiary: All landholding farmers' families having cultivable landholding in their names are eligible.
- A landholding farmer's family is defined as consisting of husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as per land records of the concerned State/UT.

3. Exclusions and Ineligibility:
- Institutional landholders.
- Farmer families wherein one or more members belong to the following categories:
  a) Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
  b) Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
  c) Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Departments, and PSUs.
  d) All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000 or more.
  e) All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
  f) Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

4. Essential Documents & Requirements:
- Land ownership records: Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTC / Pahani in Karnataka Bhoomi portal).
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory e-KYC via OTP or biometric authentication).
- Bank Account linked to Aadhaar (NPCI mapped).
- Active mobile number registered with Aadhaar.

5. Application Procedure in Karnataka & Vijayanagara District:
- Portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in (Farmers Corner > New Farmer Registration).
- Karnataka Bhoomi Portal link verifies land records automatically via FID (Farmer Identification number / FRUITS database).
- Help centers: Raitha Samparka Kendras (RSK) located in Hosapete, Harapanahalli, Kudligi, Huvina Hadagali, Hagaribommanahalli, and Kottur.
