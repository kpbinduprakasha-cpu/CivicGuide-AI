GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA
Department of Women and Child Development
OFFICIAL SCHEME GUIDELINES: GRUHA LAKSHMI YOJANA

1. Objective:
The Gruha Lakshmi scheme is a flagship social welfare initiative of the Government of Karnataka that provides financial assistance of Rs. 2,000 per month directly to the bank account of the eligible female head of the family (Gruha Lakshmi) to empower women and support household expenditures.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Domicile: The applicant woman must be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
- Family Position: The woman must be designated as the head of the family in the Antyodaya (AAY), Below Poverty Line (BPL), or Above Poverty Line (APL) ration cards.
- Single Beneficiary: Only one woman in a family is eligible for the assistance.
- Disqualification: If the woman or her husband is an Income Tax payer or files Goods and Services Tax (GST) returns, she is not eligible for the financial benefit.
- Government Employees: Women who are employed in government service or pensioners are ineligible.

3. Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card of the applicant (must be linked to bank account).
- Aadhaar Card of the husband (for verification of non-taxpayer status).
- Ration Card number (BPL, Antyodaya, or APL card issued by Food and Civil Supplies Dept, Karnataka).
- Bank Passbook with active IFSC code and bank account seeded with Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- Active mobile number linked with Aadhaar.

4. Application Process:
- Online Application: Citizens can register through the Seva Sindhu Portal (https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in) free of charge.
- Assisted Service Delivery: In Vijayanagara District and across Karnataka, applications can be submitted at Grama One, Karnataka One, Bangalore One, or Bapuji Seva Kendra.
- SMS Slot Scheduling: Beneficiaries receive an SMS with the date, time, and service centre assigned for document verification.
- Approval Authority: Department of Women and Child Development, District Office Vijayanagara at Hosapete.
- Official Portal: https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in
