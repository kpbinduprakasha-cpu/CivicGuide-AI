GUIDELINES: STATE SCHOLARSHIP PORTAL (SSP) POST-MATRIC & VIDYASIRI SCHOLARSHIPS

1. Objective:
The State Scholarship Portal (SSP) Karnataka is a unified electronic application platform for all post-matric scholarship schemes provided by the Social Welfare, Backward Classes Welfare, Tribal Welfare, Minorities Welfare, and Technical Education departments.

2. Schemes Integrated on SSP:
- Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Students (Full Fee Waiver & Maintenance).
- Vidyasiri (Food and Accommodation Scheme / Oota & Vasathi Nilaya Sahayadhana): Rs. 1,500 per month for students not admitted to government hostels.
- Fee Concession Scheme for Backward Classes (Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B).
- Chief Minister's Higher Education Scholarship.

3. Eligibility Norms:
- Student must be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
- Must have completed SSLC / 10th standard and be enrolled in recognized PUC, ITI, Diploma, Undergraduate, or Postgraduate courses in Karnataka.
- Income Limits:
  * SC / ST Students: Annual family income up to Rs. 2,50,000 for full fee reimbursement.
  * Backward Classes (Cat-1): Annual family income up to Rs. 2,50,000.
  * Backward Classes (Cat-2A, 2B, 3A, 3B): Annual family income up to Rs. 1,00,000.
- Minimum Attendance: At least 75% attendance in the enrolled educational institution.

4. Required Documents:
- Student Aadhaar Number and Aadhaar Consent Form.
- SATS (Student Achievement Tracking System) ID or College Registration Number.
- Caste and Income Certificate RD Number (issued by Revenue Dept via Nadakacheri).
- E-Authentication of Fee Receipt from the college.
- Bank Account linked with Aadhaar (NPCI active status).

5. Application Portal:
- Official URL: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in
- Common application window opens annually between August and November.
- District Social Welfare Officer, Vijayanagara District: Mini Vidhana Soudha, Hosapete.
