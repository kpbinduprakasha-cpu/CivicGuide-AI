# PM Vishwakarma Yojana (Central Government Scheme)
# Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India
# Official Portal: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in | Launch Year: 2023 | Jurisdiction: All India (Central Government)

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE AND OVERVIEW
PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme formulated to provide end-to-end holistic support to traditional artisans and craftspeople across India who work with their hands and tools. The scheme aims to enhance the quality, scale, and reach of their products while integrating them into domestic and global value chains.

2. ELIGIBLE TRADES COVERED (18 TRADITIONAL TRADES)
The scheme covers 18 traditional craftsmanship and artisan trades across all States and Union Territories:
1. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai)
2. Boat Maker
3. Armourer
4. Blacksmith (Lohar)
5. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
6. Locksmith
7. Sculptor (Moortikar/Stone Carver), Stone breaker
8. Goldsmith (Sonar)
9. Potter (Kumhaar)
10. Cobbler (Charmakar) / Shoesmith / Footwear artisan
11. Mason (Raajmistri)
12. Basket / Mat / Broom Maker / Coir Weaver
13. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
14. Barber (Naai)
15. Garland Maker (Malakaar)
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

3. CORE SCHEME BENEFITS
A. Recognition & Identity:
- Beneficiaries receive the official PM Vishwakarma Certificate and PM Vishwakarma Digital ID Card acknowledging them as certified artisans.

B. Skill Upgradation & Training:
- Basic Skill Training: 5 to 7 days (40 hours) with a daily stipend allowance of Rs. 500 per day.
- Advanced Skill Training: 15 days (120 hours) with a daily stipend allowance of Rs. 500 per day.

C. Modern Toolkit Incentive:
- Financial grant of Rs. 15,000 provided as an electronic voucher (e-RUPI) or direct bank transfer to purchase modern tools and machinery.

D. Collateral-Free Concessional Credit Support:
- First Tranche: Collateral-free enterprise development loan up to Rs. 1,00,000 at a concessional interest rate of 5% (with an interest subvention cap of 8% by Government of India) with repayment tenure of 18 months.
- Second Tranche: After successful repayment of the first loan, eligible for up to Rs. 2,00,000 at 5% concessional interest with repayment tenure of 30 months.

E. Digital Transaction Incentives:
- Rs. 1 per digital transaction incentive (up to 100 transactions per month, i.e. up to Rs. 100 monthly cash incentive) credited to the artisan's bank account.

F. Marketing Support:
- Onboarding on Government e-Marketplace (GeM), quality certification, branding, design development, and international exhibition participation.

4. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools in one of the 18 recognized family-based traditional trades.
- Minimum age of 18 years on the date of registration.
- Should be actively engaged in the relevant trade and have not availed credit under similar central or state credit-based schemes (e.g. PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra) in the past 5 years.
- Only one member per family (husband, wife, and unmarried children) is eligible to receive benefits.
- Government employees and their family members are not eligible.

5. HOW TO ENROLL AND APPLY
- Registration is free and carried out through Common Service Centres (CSCs) across India.
- Three-stage verification process:
  Stage 1: Gram Panchayat (for rural areas) or Urban Local Body (ULB) verification.
  Stage 2: District Implementation Committee (DIC) screening.
  Stage 3: National Steering Committee approval.
- Enrolled artisans can track application status online at https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in.
