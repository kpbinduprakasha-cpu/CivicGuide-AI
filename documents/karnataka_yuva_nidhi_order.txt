GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA
Skill Development, Entrepreneurship and Livelihood Department
GOVERNMENT ORDER: YUVA NIDHI SCHEME

1. Purpose and Benefit Amount:
The Yuva Nidhi scheme provides monthly financial assistance and career empowerment for educated youth of Karnataka who have completed graduation or diploma courses and remain unemployed after completing their degree.
- Degree / Post-Graduate Holders: Rs. 3,000 per month.
- Diploma Holders: Rs. 1,500 per month.
- Tenure: Benefits are provided for a maximum period of two years (24 months) or until the youth secures employment/self-employment, whichever is earlier.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Domicile: The candidate must have studied in Karnataka for at least 6 years or possess a valid Karnataka domicile.
- Qualification: Must have passed Bachelor's Degree, Post-Graduation, or Polytechnic Diploma from an institution recognized by UGC/AICTE/State Board of Technical Education.
- Unemployment Window: Must remain unemployed for a minimum of 180 days (6 months) after the declaration of final examination results.
- Employment Restriction: Candidates who are enrolled in higher full-time studies, government or private employment, or are drawing apprenticeship stipends are not eligible.
- Tax Payer Exclusion: Beneficiaries must not be income tax payees.

3. Mandatory Documents:
- Marksheets of SSLC, PUC/12th, and Degree/Diploma certificate.
- University Convocation Certificate or Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC).
- Aadhaar Card with bank seeding for DBT.
- Domicile / Study Certificate proving minimum 6 years education in Karnataka.
- Self-declaration of unemployment status on Seva Sindhu.

4. Application Channel:
- Applications are submitted online via Seva Sindhu portal (https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in) or at nearest Grama One kendras in taluks of Hosapete, Harapanahalli, Kudligi, Huvina Hadagali, Hagaribommanahalli, and Kottur.
- Beneficiaries must update monthly self-declaration of unemployment between the 25th and last day of every month to receive subsequent installments.
